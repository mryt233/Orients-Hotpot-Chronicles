Welcome to **Orient's Hotpot Chronicles**, a Fabric mod that brings **sharing a hotpot meal** into Minecraft. Build a stove, set a copper or iron pot on top, pour in broth, add tripe and fatty beef rolls, then pick up a bite with chopsticks, dip it in your own sauce, and enjoy.

This guide has two parts: **learning to play** and **looking things up**.

## What's in the mod?

- **Stoves and hotpots:** short and tall stoves; copper and iron nine-grid pots (Yuanyang and four-grid pots are coming in 0.1.3); four broths; cooking with wood, coal, or a campfire.
- **Tables and seating:** low and tall hotpot tables, stools, and benches in 11 wood types. Tables connect automatically, and you can sit on the seating.
- **Tableware:** five materials (quartz, bamboo, terracotta, copper, and iron) in six plate sizes. Vanilla bowls can hold food or soup on the table. There are also two drinks and bamboo chopsticks.
- **Ingredients:** tripe, fatty beef rolls, mutton rolls, potato slices, tofu cubes, meatballs, and more. Prepare them yourself with a cutting board, kitchen knife, and rolling pin.
- **Dipping sauces:** a sauce station with nine seasonings. Mix your own sauce and give dipped food extra effects.
- **Eat together:** a heated pot keeps healing nearby players. Gathering around a hotpot really does have its benefits.

## Supported versions and downloads

The mod supports **Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge) and 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge)**. **This guide demonstrates 1.21.1 Fabric.** Download links (CurseForge, Modrinth, GitHub), installation steps, and feedback channels (GitHub and QQ) are on [Downloads, Installation & Feedback](install.html).

:::warn
The mod is still in **alpha** and has only had preliminary testing. When upgrading worlds from 0.1.0-alpha.1, modded items may disappear or change. **Back up your world first**; a new world is recommended.
:::
