# Minecraft [Orient's Hotpot Chronicles] Mod — Player Guide

:::tip
**This guide covers released 0.2.2-alpha, demonstrated with Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric** (1.21.1 NeoForge, 1.20.1 Fabric / Forge, and 26.1.x & 26.2 Fabric / NeoForge play the same; see [Downloads, Installation & Feedback](install.html)). This release adds Crispy Pork, Dried Tofu, Tofu Skin, Wide Potato Noodles, Sweet Potato Skin, Enoki Mushrooms, Bok Choy, Lotus Root Slices, Squid Rolls, Egg Fried Rice, Chili Peppers, Houttuynia Root, and hot/cold Tofu, expands seasonings to 15, and introduces 4-level Spicy Red Broth, six broth flavor bonuses, tabletop chopsticks with mini porcelain rests, stackable bench ladders, cup toasting, the Cloth Apron, in-book guide search, a mod menu settings screen, and JEI / REI & Jade support.

Welcome to **Orient's Hotpot Chronicles**, a Fabric mod that brings **sharing a hotpot meal** into Minecraft. Build a stove, set a copper or iron pot on top, pour in broth, add tripe and fatty beef rolls, then pick up a bite with chopsticks, dip it in your own sauce, and enjoy.

This guide has two parts: **learning to play** and **looking things up**.

## What's in the mod?

- **Stoves and hotpots:** low and tall stoves; copper and iron pots (nine-grid, Yuanyang, and four-grid pots); 6 broths; cook with wood, coal, or over a campfire.
- **Tables and seating:** low and tall hotpot tables in 11 woods and 9 stone / metal materials, with matching stools and benches. Tables connect automatically, and you can sit on the seating.
- **Tableware:** five materials (quartz, bamboo, terracotta, copper, and iron) in six plate sizes. Vanilla bowls can hold food or soup on the table. There are also three drinks, glass cups, and bamboo chopsticks.
- **Ingredients:** tripe, fatty beef rolls, mutton rolls, loin slices, shrimp paste, ham sausages, potato slices, tofu cubes, meatballs, and more. Prepare them yourself with a cutting board, kitchen knife, and rolling pin.
- **Dipping sauces:** a sauce station with 15 seasonings. Mix your own sauce and give dipped food extra effects.
- **Eat together:** a heated pot keeps healing nearby players. Gathering around a hotpot really does have its benefits.

## The in-game Hotpot Guide {#guide}{{since:0.1.4-alpha}}

{{recipe:hotpot_guide}}

Shapelessly craft an **Iron Kitchen Knife + book** into a Hotpot Guide (the knife is consumed). Right-click it to open the red book. Choose a category on the left and an entry at the top (scroll the mouse wheel or drag horizontally when there are many entries), or click the top-right compass (`Ctrl + F` / `/`) to search the whole book. Use arrows, direction keys, or the mouse wheel to turn pages; reopening returns to the last page. It includes all 9 chapters, 56 entries, tables, and recipes from this book tutorial.
