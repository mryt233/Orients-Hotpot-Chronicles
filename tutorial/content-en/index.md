Welcome to **Orient's Hotpot Chronicles**, a Fabric mod that brings **sharing a hotpot meal** into Minecraft. Build a stove, set a copper or iron pot on top, pour in broth, add tripe and fatty beef rolls, then pick up a bite with chopsticks, dip it in your own sauce, and enjoy.

This guide has two parts: **learning to play** and **looking things up**.

## What's in the mod?

- **Stoves and hotpots:** low and tall stoves; copper and iron pots (nine-grid, Yuanyang, and four-grid pots); 6 broths; cook with wood, coal, or over a campfire.
- **Tables and seating:** low and tall hotpot tables in 11 woods and 9 stone / metal materials, with matching stools and benches. Tables connect automatically, and you can sit on the seating.
- **Tableware:** five materials (quartz, bamboo, terracotta, copper, and iron) in six plate sizes. Vanilla bowls can hold food or soup on the table. There are also three drinks, glass cups, and bamboo chopsticks.
- **Ingredients:** tripe, fatty beef rolls, mutton rolls, loin slices, shrimp paste, potato slices, tofu cubes, meatballs, and more. Prepare them yourself with a cutting board, kitchen knife, and rolling pin.
- **Dipping sauces:** a sauce station with 10 seasonings. Mix your own sauce and give dipped food extra effects.
- **Eat together:** a heated pot keeps healing nearby players. Gathering around a hotpot really does have its benefits.

## The in-game Hotpot Guide {#guide}{{since:0.1.4-alpha}}

{{recipe:hotpot_guide}}

Shapelessly craft an **Iron Kitchen Knife + book** into a Hotpot Guide (the knife is consumed). Right-click it to open the red book. Choose a category on the left and an entry at the top. Use arrows, direction keys, or the mouse wheel to turn pages; reopening returns to the last page. It contains concise instructions and recipes; this website gives the fuller explanation.

## Supported versions and downloads

The mod supports **Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge) and 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge)**. **This guide demonstrates 1.21.1 Fabric.** Download links (CurseForge, Modrinth, GitHub), installation steps, and feedback channels (GitHub and QQ) are on [Downloads, Installation & Feedback](install.html).

:::warn
The mod is still in **alpha** and has only had preliminary testing. When upgrading worlds from 0.1.0-alpha.1, modded items may disappear or change. **Back up your world first**; a new world is recommended.
:::
