# Ingredients & Food Preparation

Ingredients make the meal. This chapter explains **where to find them**, **how to prepare them**, and **how much hunger they restore when cooked**.

{{icons:tripe,fatty_beef_roll,mutton_roll,beef_loin_slice,potato_slice,cold_tofu_cube,beef_meatball,fish_ball,scallion,cilantro,soybean}}

:::tip
**All raw ingredients can be cooked in the pot**, or in a furnace, smoker, or campfire. The tables below list hunger restored per cooked portion. **Cooked food cannot go back into the pot.**
:::

## Tripe {#tripe}

{{icons:tripe,cooked_tripe}}

- **Source:** cows and mooshrooms drop it using the same quantity rules as vanilla beef: 1–3 pieces, plus 0–1 per Looting level. Animals killed while burning drop cooked tripe.
- **Raw Tripe** restores two hunger points; **Cooked Tripe** restores five.
- Great for hotpot: about 2.5 seconds to cook in the pot (half the time of a whole piece of beef).

{{recipe:cooked_tripe}}

## Beef Loin and Beef Loin Slices {#loin}{{since:0.1.3-alpha-hotfix1}}

{{icons:beef_loin,cooked_beef_loin,beef_loin_slice,cooked_beef_loin_slice}}

- **Beef Loin:** cows / mooshrooms have a **25%** chance to drop an extra piece (+10% per Looting level; burning animals drop Cooked Beef Loin). Eaten raw, it restores three hunger points; cooking it whole produces **Cooked Beef Loin**, restoring eight, just like vanilla steak.
- **Beef Loin Slices**, the hotpot cut known as *diaolong*: cut a loin with a kitchen knife on a [cutting board](#board), or use a **stonecutter**, to get **three slices**. Each restores one hunger point raw or three cooked.
- Loin slices **cook fastest in the pot** (twice as fast as fatty beef rolls), finishing in just a few seconds.
- Cooked Beef Loin cannot be sliced.

{{recipe:beef_loin_slice,cooked_beef_loin}}

## Cutting board, kitchen knife, and rolling pin {#board}{{since:0.1.2-alpha}}

Craft these three tools before preparing ingredients:

### Cutting board

:::tip
**Directional placement**, **visibility while held**, **mining only the food on top**, and **brighter inventory icons** are all improvements introduced in 0.1.3-alpha-hotfix1. In 0.1.2-alpha, the board has no facing direction, is barely visible in hand, and breaks together with the food on it.
:::

{{recipe:cutting_board}}

- **Two logs of any type placed side by side horizontally** yield **two cutting boards** (any wood type).
- **Directional placement:** the board follows your placement direction and food faces you.
- **Hold something that can be cut or pounded and right-click the board** to place one item. **Right-click with an empty hand** to take it back.
- **Mine the food on top** to remove just that item in one hit, leaving the board.

### Iron Kitchen Knife

{{recipe:iron_kitchen_knife}}

A square-tipped Chinese cleaver with 250 durability. **It also works as a weapon**, dealing four damage.

### Rolling Pin

{{recipe:rolling_pin}}

Has 120 durability and is used to **pound meatballs**.

## Cutting ingredients {#cut}{{since:0.1.2-alpha}}

Place an ingredient on the board, then **right-click with the Iron Kitchen Knife** to cut:

| On the board | Cuts needed | Output | Hunger per cooked portion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{i:minecraft:beef}} Raw Beef | 1 | 3 {{i:fatty_beef_roll}} | 3 |
| {{i:minecraft:mutton}} Raw Mutton | 1 | 3 {{i:mutton_roll}} | 3 |
| {{i:beef_loin}} | 1 | 3 {{i:beef_loin_slice}} | 3 |
| {{i:minecraft:potato}} Potato | 3 cuts, 1 portion per cut | 3 portions of {{i:potato_slice}} (three slices in each portion) | 2 |
| {{i:cold_tofu}} Tofu (Cold) | 1 | 3 portions of {{i:cold_tofu_cube}} | 2 |
| {{i:hot_tofu}} Tofu (Hot) | 1 | 3 portions of {{i:hot_tofu_cube}} (already cooked) | 2 |

**Shortcut:** use a **stonecutter** to prepare the whole batch at once.

:::warn
In 0.1.2-alpha, **potatoes and tofu cannot be placed on the cutting board** due to a known issue (use a stonecutter instead). This is fixed in 0.1.3-alpha-hotfix1.
:::

## Pounding meatballs {#meatball}{{since:0.1.2-alpha}}

**Suitable ingredients:** raw beef, porkchops, mutton, cod, and salmon.

1. Place the meat on the board.
2. **Right-click the board with a Rolling Pin three times:**
   - First hit: crush the meat.
   - Second hit: turn it into a paste.
   - Third hit: produce **three raw meatballs**.
3. The meatballs are small 3D spheres. Cook them in the pot; each cooked meatball restores **three hunger points**.

| Ingredient | Raw meatball |
| --- | --- |
| Raw beef | {{i:beef_meatball}} |
| Raw porkchop | {{i:pork_meatball}} |
| Raw mutton | {{i:mutton_meatball}} |
| Raw cod / salmon | {{i:fish_ball}} |

### Cilantro meatballs

Combine two raw meatballs with one {{i:cilantro}} in a shapeless crafting recipe to get **two** cilantro meatballs. All four meatball types have cilantro variants. Each cooked one restores **four hunger points**, more than a plain meatball.

{{recipe:cilantro_beef_meatball}}

## Shrimp, peeled shrimp, and shrimp paste {#shrimp}{{since:0.1.4-alpha}}

{{icons:shrimp,peeled_shrimp,shrimp_paste}}

- **Shrimp:** caught while fishing, roughly one in six fish catches.
- **Peeled Shrimp:** cut a shrimp with a knife on the board, or use a stonecutter.
- **Shrimp Paste:** pound peeled shrimp on the board with a rolling pin 3 times.
- All three can be cooked in a pot or roasted. Cooked portions restore **3 / 3 / 4** hunger points respectively. Cooked Shrimp and Cooked Peeled Shrimp also have a small flavor line in their tooltips.

{{recipe:peeled_shrimp,cooked_shrimp_paste}}

## Minced pork and ham sausages {#sausage}{{since:0.2.0-alpha}}

{{icons:minced_pork,raw_ham_sausage,ham_sausage,ham_sausage_slice,cooked_ham_sausage_slice}}

1. Place a **raw porkchop** on the cutting board and cut it once with an iron kitchen knife to get **1 Minced Pork**; a stonecutter also works. (Pounding pork meatballs with the rolling pin is unchanged.)
2. Craft **Minced Pork + 1 vanilla wheat** (shapeless) to get **3 Raw Ham Sausages**.
3. Cook Raw Ham Sausages in a pot, furnace, smoker, or campfire to get **Ham Sausages**.
4. Place a **Ham Sausage** on the cutting board and cut it once with a knife for **3 Ham Sausage Slices**; a stonecutter also works. Raw ham sausages cannot be cut on the board.
5. **Slices can be heated once more** (pot, furnace, smoker, or campfire) to become **Cooked Ham Sausage Slices**; these cannot go back into the pot.

{{recipe:minced_pork,raw_ham_sausage,ham_sausage,ham_sausage_slice,cooked_ham_sausage_slice}}

:::tip
Remember the order: **Raw Porkchop → Minced Pork → Raw Ham Sausage → Ham Sausage → Ham Sausage Slice → Cooked Ham Sausage Slice**. Minced Pork is a crafting ingredient and cannot be eaten directly.
:::

## Tofu and soybeans {#tofu}{{since:0.1.2-alpha}}

- **Soybeans:** breaking **short or tall grass** without shears has a **15%** chance to drop one.
- **Tofu (Cold):** shapelessly craft three soybeans and one water bucket into a block of cold tofu. **The bucket is returned.**
- One knife cut turns cold tofu on a board into three **Tofu Cubes (Cold)**, each restoring two hunger points when cooked.

{{recipe:cold_tofu}}

:::tip
**Since 0.2.2-alpha, tofu comes hot or cold** (in 0.2.1-alpha and earlier it was "Tofu", "Tofu Cubes" and "Cooked Tofu Cubes", and a whole block of tofu stayed the same item after cooking):

{{icons:cold_tofu,hot_tofu,hot_tofu_cube}}

- Simmer a whole **Tofu (Cold)** in the pot for **10 seconds** and it becomes **Tofu (Hot)**. It is cooked: pick it up with chopsticks or by hand, but it **cannot go back into the pot**.
- Leave the hot tofu in the pot and, **30 seconds** after it went in, it **breaks into Tofu Cubes (Hot)**, the same number of portions. Grab it early if you want the whole block.
- Hot tofu can also be cut on the board into three **Tofu Cubes (Hot)**. Cold tofu cubes cook into hot tofu cubes in a furnace, smoker, campfire or the pot.
- Server owners can change both times in the config file: `cooking.tofu_cook_ticks` (cooking, default 200 ticks = 10 s) and `cooking.tofu_overcook_ticks` (breaking apart, counted from when it went in, default 600 ticks = 30 s). Run `/orientshotpot reload` to apply.
:::

## Crispy pork {#crispy_pork}{{since:0.2.2-alpha}}

{{icons:raw_crispy_pork,crispy_pork,boiled_crispy_pork}}

Freshly fried crispy pork is the first plate to be emptied at any hotpot table.

1. Combine **minced pork + vanilla wheat + 1 egg** (shapeless, anywhere in the crafting grid) to get **3 portions of Raw Crispy Pork**, restoring 1 hunger point. Still raw batter and peppercorn — don't eat it yet.
2. Cook raw crispy pork in a **furnace (10 s) / smoker (5 s) / campfire (30 s)**, or drop it **straight into the pot**, and you get **Crispy Pork**, restoring **4 hunger points**. Golden and crackling, best eaten with your fingers.
3. Freshly fried crispy pork can go **back into the pot for a second swim**: with coal and clear broth it takes about **7.5 seconds** to turn into **Boiled Crispy Pork**, restoring **6 hunger points**. Crisp outside, tender inside, and full of broth.
4. Boiled crispy pork is the end of the chain — **it cannot go back into the pot** (you'll get a hint), so enjoy it while it is hot.

{{recipe:raw_crispy_pork,crispy_pork}}

:::tip
Remember the order: **Raw Porkchop → Minced Pork → Raw Crispy Pork → Crispy Pork → Boiled Crispy Pork**. Want both textures? Fry half to eat straight away and save half for the pot.
:::

## Dried tofu, tofu skin, and sweet potato skin {#tofu_products_noodles}{{since:0.2.2-alpha}}

{{icons:dried_tofu,cooked_dried_tofu,tofu_skin,cooked_tofu_skin,wide_noodles,cooked_wide_noodles,sweet_potato_skin,cooked_sweet_potato_skin}}

Cold tofu and potato slices are good for more than the pot — press them and cut them and you get four hotpot favorites that hold broth beautifully:

| Make this | How to lay it out | Yield | Raw | Cooked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{i:dried_tofu}} | 2 **cold tofu** side by side **in a row** | 3 | 2 | 4 |
| {{i:tofu_skin}} | 2 **cold tofu** stacked **in a column** | 3 | 2 | 4 |
| {{i:wide_noodles}} | 2 **potato slices** side by side **in a row** | 3 | 1 | 4 |
| {{i:sweet_potato_skin}} | 2 **potato slices** stacked **in a column** | 3 | 1 | 4 |

- **Dried Tofu** is pressed firm and gets more bean flavour the longer you chew; **Tofu Skin** is dry and softens in seconds, so every fold soaks up broth.
- **Wide Potato Noodles** are slippery — hold them with chopsticks carefully or they slide back into the pot; **Sweet Potato Skin** carries a hint of sweetness.
- All four can be simmered in the pot or baked in a furnace, smoker or campfire. In the pot they take about **5–7.5 seconds**: tofu skin and sweet potato skin 5 s, dried tofu and wide potato noodles 7.5 s.

{{recipe:dried_tofu,tofu_skin,wide_noodles,sweet_potato_skin,cooked_dried_tofu,cooked_tofu_skin,cooked_wide_noodles,cooked_sweet_potato_skin}}

:::tip
A row and a column are two different recipes: cold tofu in a row gives **Dried Tofu**, in a column gives **Tofu Skin**; potato slices in a row give **Wide Potato Noodles**, in a column give **Sweet Potato Skin**. Lay them the wrong way and you get something else.
:::

## Mushrooms, vegetables and seafood {#veggies_seafood}{{since:0.2.2-alpha}}

{{icons:enoki_mushroom,cooked_enoki_mushroom,greens,cooked_greens,lotus_root,lotus_root_slice,cooked_lotus_root_slice,raw_squid,squid_roll,cooked_squid_roll}}

- **Enoki Mushroom**: craft 2 with 2 brown mushrooms, or break **brown / red mushrooms** themselves for a 50% chance to get a handful. They cook in **2.5 seconds**; **Cooked Enoki Mushroom** restores 3 hunger points — thin strands that drink up the broth.
- **Bok Choy**: breaking short or tall grass has a **15%** chance to drop one bunch, no shears needed. Simmer a whole bunch for **2.5 seconds** for **Cooked Bok Choy**, restoring 3 hunger points; the freshest bite in a table full of red broth.
- **Lotus Root**: breaking **lily pads** has a **50%** chance to drop a segment (shears will not get it). A whole segment cannot go into the pot — put it on the [cutting board](#board) and cut **3 times, one slice per cut**, or use a stonecutter to get **3 Lotus Root Slices** at once. Slices cook in **5 seconds**; **Cooked Lotus Root Slices** restore 3 hunger points, crisp and sweet, with broth hidden in every hole.
- **Raw Squid**: squid and glow squid drop 1–2 (plus 0–1 per Looting level). A whole squid cannot go into the pot either — one knife cut (or a stonecutter) gives **3 Squid Rolls**, already scored in a wheat-ear pattern; **5 seconds** in the pot turns them into **Cooked Squid Rolls**, restoring 4 hunger points, curling up as they cook.
- All of these can be baked too: furnace 10 s, smoker 5 s, campfire 30 s — baked and simmered taste the same.

{{recipe:enoki_mushroom,lotus_root_slice,squid_roll,cooked_enoki_mushroom,cooked_greens,cooked_lotus_root_slice,cooked_squid_roll}}

:::tip
Enoki mushrooms and bok choy are in the **fastest** tier (as quick as beef loin slices and tripe); lotus root slices and squid rolls are medium; dried tofu and wide potato noodles a little slower. Trying to put a whole lotus root or a whole raw squid in the pot tells you to cut it first.
:::

## Egg fried rice {#egg_fried_rice}{{since:0.2.2-alpha}}

{{icons:egg_fried_rice}}

1. Combine **1 vanilla wheat + 1 egg + 1 scallion + 1 bowl** (shapeless, anywhere in the crafting grid) to get **1 bowl of Egg Fried Rice**.
2. It restores **7 hunger points** — golden egg around every grain, a scatter of green scallion, perfect for cooling down a spicy broth.
3. Egg fried rice is a **finished dish**: it does not go into the pot, and the bowl is part of the recipe, so the whole thing is eaten in one go.

{{recipe:egg_fried_rice}}

## Chili peppers {#chili_pepper}{{since:0.2.2-alpha}}

{{icons:chili_pepper,millet_chili}}

1. Breaking **short or tall grass** has a **15%** chance to drop a **Chili Pepper**, no shears needed.
2. It **can be eaten directly** (1 hunger point) for a clean hit of heat, or placed on the [cutting board](#board) and cut once with a knife (or a stonecutter) for **2 portions of Chopped Millet Chili**.
3. **Chopped Millet Chili** is the 14th seasoning at the [sauce station](sauce.html#seasoning): anything dipped in it gives **Strength I for 8 seconds**.
4. Most importantly, chili peppers **turn up the heat of spicy red broth**: hold a chili pepper and **right-click the red broth area** to raise the heat level, up to Extreme Spicy; ice brings it back down. See [Stoves & Pots](hotpot.html#soup).

{{recipe:millet_chili}}

:::warn
**Neither chili peppers nor chopped millet chili go into the pot**: clicking the pot with them only changes the heat level or shows a hint, and they are never treated as an ingredient. For heat in your food, raise the level of the red broth.
:::

## Houttuynia root {#zhe_er_gen}{{since:0.2.2-alpha}}

{{icons:zhe_er_gen,zhe_er_gen_slice,cooked_zhe_er_gen_slice,minced_zhe_er_gen}}

1. Punching **short grass / the lower half of tall grass** (no shears) has a **15%** chance to drop a **Houttuynia Root** (the root of fish mint) — the same odds as bok choy and peanuts.
2. You can **eat it raw** (restores 1 hunger point): crisp, pale white and jointed, with that fishy aroma people from the southwest know and love.
3. Put the whole root on the [cutting board](#board) and hit it once with the kitchen knife (or use a stonecutter) to get **3 Houttuynia Root Slices**. The whole root cannot go into the pot — slice it first.
4. Throw **Houttuynia Root Slices** into the hotpot for **10 seconds (200 ticks)** to get **Cooked Houttuynia Root Slices** (restores 3 hunger points). A furnace, smoker or campfire also cooks them.
5. You can also pound the whole root on the cutting board with the **rolling pin**, **3 hits**: hit 1 is still the root, from hit 2 on it turns into a little pile of mince, and hit 3 drops **2 Minced Houttuynia**.
6. **Minced Houttuynia** is the 15th seasoning on the [sauce station](sauce.html#seasoning): dip a dish in it and you get **Resistance I for 10 seconds** plus **poison cure** — houttuynia is a cooling, detoxifying herb after all.

{{recipe:zhe_er_gen_slice,cooked_zhe_er_gen_slice}}

:::tip
**Most of the fishy smell cooks off**: raw houttuynia is pungent; after a 10-second dip only a faint sweet aftertaste is left. If you are new to it, blanching it in the pot first is the safest way in.
:::

## Scallions and cilantro {#garnish}{{since:0.1.2-alpha}}

- Break **short grass or the lower half of tall grass** without shears. Scallions and cilantro each have a **25%** drop chance.
- Both **can be eaten raw** for one hunger point. They are also [dipping sauce](sauce.html) ingredients, and cilantro can make cilantro meatballs.

- Grass also drops **tomatoes** for hotpot base, **garlic** for garlic paste, and **soybeans** for tofu.
- **Cooked Kelp:** cook vanilla kelp in a hotpot to restore 2 hunger points. Furnaces, smokers, and campfires still produce dried kelp.
