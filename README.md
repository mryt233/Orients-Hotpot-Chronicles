# Orient's Hotpot Chronicles · 之晗的火锅物语

A Chongqing-style hotpot for Minecraft. Build a brick stove, seat an iron or copper pot on it, pour in a broth, light the fire, and cook your food in a bubbling 9×9-grid pot. Then eat straight out of the pot with chopsticks.

Everything uses vanilla materials and vanilla-style textures, so it fits right into a normal survival world.

## Features

- **Brick Stove**: fuel it like a furnace. Animated fire at the stove mouth, sparks rising around the pot.
- **Iron & Copper Pots**: seat them on the stove, or place them on the ground.
- **Four broths**:
  - Clear (water bucket)
  - Milky (milk bucket)
  - Mushroom (mushroom stew)
  - Spicy (lava bucket, don't ask)

  Scoop the broth back out with an empty bucket or bowl.
- **A 9×9 cooking grid (81 slots)**: drop in raw food and take it out cooked, with furnace XP. The broth boils with cube bubbles and steam, and ingredients bob in the soup.
- **Chopsticks**: left-click cooked food in the pot to eat it on the spot. Eating from a cold pot may make you nauseous, so eat it while it's hot!
- **Warm up around the pot**: a lit stove with a pot gives Regeneration I to players within 6 blocks.
- **No wasted fuel**: the stove only burns fuel while there is raw food to cook.
- **Take the whole pot with you**: mine the pot and it keeps its broth and food. Place it down anywhere and everything is still there.
- **Hotpot tables** in 11 wood types. They connect into one big table around the stove.
- **Benches** in every wood type. Benches placed side by side join into one long bench.
- **Plates** in quartz, bamboo, terracotta, copper and iron, in several sizes, to serve food on the table.
- **10 languages**: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português (BR).

- Minecraft **1.21.1**
- Fabric Loader

## 中文介绍

在 MC 里吃一顿重庆火锅：砌好砖灶台，把铁锅或铜锅坐上去，倒进汤底，点火，生食材丢进九宫格大锅里涮，熟了直接用筷子夹着吃。全部用原版材料合成，贴图也是原版风格。

- **砖灶台**：像熔炉一样烧燃料，灶口有火焰动画，锅边会冒火星。
- **铁锅、铜锅**：可以坐在灶台上，也可以单独放在地上。
- **四种汤底**：清汤（水桶）、浓汤（牛奶桶）、菌汤（蘑菇煲）、红汤（岩浆桶）。倒进去之后，还能用空桶或碗舀回来。
- **81 格大锅**：生食材放进去煮熟，拿出来有经验。汤会翻滚冒方块气泡和热气，食材在汤里上下浮动。
- **筷子**：左键直接夹锅里的熟食吃。锅凉了吃可能会反胃，要趁热吃！
- **围锅回血**：灶台点着火、上面有锅时，6 格内的玩家获得生命恢复。
- **不浪费燃料**：只有锅里有生食材要煮时才烧燃料。
- **整锅端走**：挖下来的锅连汤带菜，放到哪里都原样还在。
- **火锅桌**：11 种木头，自动拼成围着灶台的一整张桌子。
- **条凳**：每种木头都有，挨着放会连成长凳。
- **盘子**：石英、竹编、红陶、铜、铁五种材质，有多种尺寸，用来上菜摆桌。
- **支持 10 种语言**。
